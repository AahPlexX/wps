# World Political Structures product context

World Political Structures is a public reference site for comparing the formal top political assignment, government structure, and simplified national hierarchy of 195 sovereign states. The audience includes general readers, students, educators, researchers, and anyone who needs a fast structural comparison without conflating monarchy with dictatorship.

The primary task is finding a country and understanding its political structure. The directory supports broad comparison; the 3D globe supports geographic exploration. Both surfaces use the same local political dataset and expose the same country facts.

The public site does not expose external source links or citations. Research provenance is maintained in repository documentation instead.
